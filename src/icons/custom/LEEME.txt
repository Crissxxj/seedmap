Se pueden reemplazar los iconos pixel-art de la app:
copia aquí tus PNG (los que quieras) nombrados con la clave del icono.
Claves válidas (mira array KEYLIST en src/icons.js):
  village_plains, village_desert, village_savanna, village_taiga, village_snowy, village_zombie,
  desert_pyramid, jungle_temple, witch_hut, igloo, igloo_basement, monument, mansion, outpost,
  ruined_portal, ruined_portal_n, ruined_portal_giant, ancient_city, trail_ruins, trial_chamber,
  shipwreck, ocean_ruin_warm, ocean_ruin_cold, treasure, fortress,
  bastion_housing, bastion_hoglin, bastion_treasure, bastion_bridge, end_city
Ejemplo: village_plains.png reemplaza el icono de la aldea de llanura (mapa + listas + chips).
